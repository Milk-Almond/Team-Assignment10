# NYU stockroom feasibility review

**Decision for senior management.** Should the hypothetical NYU stockroom team prepare a comparison of lightweight tote placement, and is there enough evidence to begin a physical trial? The case uses the team's November 17, 2026 evidence snapshot. These two decisions have different evidence requirements.

## Findings from the seven runs

| Order and specialist | Finding that matters to management |
|---|---|
| 1 · [Creation](01_creation.json) | Walking the route and placing a tote securely are distinct capabilities. Compare correctly completed placements, human handoffs, interventions and staff time under the same task conditions. The packet supports a partial commercial history and leaves the original development lineage uncertain. |
| 2 · [Innovation Classification](02_innovation.json) | The proposed stockroom change is prospective incremental process innovation. The workflow still moves the same totes to the same shelves. A reduced handoff matters only if supervision and recovery consume less effort than they save. |
| 3 · [Promethean Classification](03_promethean.json) | The specialist provisionally classifies the general capability as a breakthrough under the course framework, with limited confidence in that interpretation. Its own credible alternative is incremental automation of bounded tasks. Evidence of broad societal transformation is insufficient. |
| 4 · [Diffusion](04_diffusion.json) | The industrial examples document some use. At both the general and campus levels, the packet lacks representative, dated evidence of who adopted and when. The expert therefore abstains from estimating adoption share, assigning a Rogers adopter category or forecasting diffusion speed at either level. It finds no evidence of a peer-driven deadline for NYU. |
| 5 · [Adoption](05_adoption.json) | **Defer physical execution.** Facilities can design the comparison and obtain permission and quotes. Reconsider a bounded experiment once the site, operator, safety and data approvals and trial limits are documented. |
| 6 · [Organizational Adoption](06_organizational.json) | **Request evidence.** Operating ownership, stop authority, backup coverage, site clearance and operator competence remain unverified. | 7 · [Technology Landscape](07_landscape.json) | Compare complete services through stable placement on the assigned shelf. A wheeled robot needs a human handoff. Compare staff minutes and cost per accepted placement, including setup, supervision, failures and recovery. Record safety events separately. |

## Synthesized recommendation for human review

**Design the comparison now and defer the powered trial.** In this hypothetical NYU case, the stockroom, participating staff and access to a robot are unconfirmed (C1). First identify a candidate stockroom and a person authorized to grant access. If a facilities operations lead takes ownership and observation is permitted, prioritize the cart baseline: measure staff time, handoffs and stable placements on a shelf identified for the comparison. If access remains unresolved, report that gap at review. Paper planning and requests for quotes can proceed. Prepare a costed plan for a supervised humanoid and, if feasible, a wheeled robot with a human handoff. Specify a common proposed route, tote loads, shelf destinations and placement criteria for any later authorized comparison.

**Why the labels differ.** Innovation classifies the local workflow change as incremental. Promethean classifies the general capability as a provisional breakthrough and also preserves an incremental interpretation. The first two labels concern different scopes; the uncertainty about general significance is genuine. Neither resolves local value or readiness. Adoption's deferral and Organizational Adoption's evidence request fit together: one specifies the current action and the other identifies evidence that could reopen it.

**Proposed review appointment.** Once a facilities operations lead is confirmed, that person should bring the design, dated quotes, cart-baseline results or access status, and outstanding approvals to a first review targeted for **December 17, 2026**, or earlier if the evidence is ready. This date comes from the Adoption specialist. At intake, identify a potential site sponsor and ask them to confirm the named trial decision-maker and required signatories. The proposed recipient is the facilities director, with safety review by Environmental Health and Safety. NYU’s actual delegation and the appointment still need confirmation.

**Evidence to bring before reconsidering a powered trial**

| Review item | What the reviewer can check |
|---|---|
| Permission and accountability | Written site, safety and data permissions for the proposed task. Identify the trained operator, backup, person authorized to stop the trial and person authorized to restart it. |
| Equipment fit | Compare documented aisle width, shelf height and tote dimensions and loaded weight with the proposed equipment’s clearance, reach and load limits. Flag missing measurements. |
| Spending limit | The facilities lead obtains dated quotes including setup, supervision and support. The budget owner records an approved monetary cap and checks the proposed cost against it. |
| Comparison criteria | Record the cart baseline and proposed thresholds for stable placement, staff time and cost per successful placement. Agree on stop conditions before testing. A missing baseline stays visible in the review. |

The authorized reviewer may accept, modify, reject or request more evidence. A calendar date brings the decision back for review. Physical testing still requires documented authorization. The [management team review](team-review.md) records acceptance of planning and deferral of the powered trial.

## Evidence and verification

The [case](case.json) and [evidence register](evidence-register.md) contain R1–R3 (industrial announcements), R4 (safety guidance) and C1 (hypothetical local conditions). The source snapshot was held fixed; the underlying external claims were not independently refreshed for this exercise. No site approval, comparative performance data or campus-wide diffusion estimate was available.

Seven isolated Codex specialist sessions ran in the order shown. Each received the common case and its pinned instructions. Every saved response passed the local structural check against the frozen output contract and matched its pinned specialist identity. The [run manifest](manifest.json) identifies each source version, packet and response hash. This is one observed case. The team accepted the recommendation. Repeated runs and the other two planned integration cases remain open.
