# Management team review of the NYU stockroom recommendation

**Decision: Accept.** “Design the comparison now and defer the powered trial.”

The team reported reviewing the supplied brief. Designing the comparison is low-cost and reversible. The work involves documenting the cart baseline, obtaining quotes and agreeing on what counts as a successful placement. Site approval, safety and data permissions, a named operator and backup, stop authority and comparative performance data remain outstanding. The team accepted deferral of powered testing until the documented approvals and comparison criteria can be reviewed.

The team found the incremental stockroom classification and the provisional breakthrough classification compatible because they concern different scopes. Neither establishes local value or readiness. Adoption’s deferral identifies the current action. Organizational Adoption’s evidence request identifies what would reopen it.

**Concern and follow-up.** One member questioned whether “if permitted, measure the baseline” gave the cart comparison enough priority. The team agreed to ask the facilities lead to prioritize measuring it, subject to permission. That baseline is the reference for judging the humanoid and wheeled alternatives. The team retained its decision to accept.
